Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifications do I need to start a consulting practice? expand_more

Successful consultants combine deep technical expertise (5-10+ years hands-on experience), business acumen, and communication skills. Formal credentials like Cicerone or Master Brewers certifications add credibility but aren't strictly required. The highest-paid consultants have commercial brewing experience managing actual production facilities. Start with smaller projects while building your portfolio.

How do I price consulting services competitively? expand_more

Research market rates ($150-$400/hour based on experience). Project-based pricing provides budget certainty and incentivizes efficiency. Calculate by estimating hours required and adding 20-30% buffer. Value-based pricing works when results are measurable. Start conservatively and raise rates 15-25% annually as demand grows.

How do I manage liability and protect myself legally? expand_more

Professional liability insurance is essential ($1,500-$5,000 annually for $1-2M coverage). Use comprehensive engagement agreements specifying scope and liability limitations. Clearly delineate advisory services from guaranteed outcomes. Form an LLC for additional protection and maintain detailed documentation of recommendations.

Should I specialize or offer broad consulting services? expand_more

Most successful consultants start with specific expertise (recipe development, equipment selection) then expand. Deep specialization creates competitive differentiation and allows premium pricing. Broader offerings attract more clients but require diverse expertise. Test market demand before committing to narrow niche.

How do I handle clients who don't follow recommendations? expand_more

Document all recommendations with rationale and risks. Use agreements specifying that you provide advice but client retains decision authority. When clients disregard significant recommendations, document in writing to protect yourself legally. Prioritize recommendations (critical vs. nice-to-have) and explain consequences clearly.

What's realistic timeline to build sustainable consulting income? expand_more

Building to full-time income ($80K-$150K+) typically requires 18-36 months. First 6 months: foundation building and landing first clients. Months 7-18: delivery excellence and building referrals. By months 18-24: 3-5 active clients with steady pipeline. Plan for 6 months cash reserves before going full-time.
